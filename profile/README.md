**Your band's songbook – on every device, on every stage, even without Wi-Fi.**

setDING shows your band the chords of its songs during a performance. The band
leader puts the set together once; every member has it on their own tablet,
phone or computer – the right songs, in the right order, in the right key – and
plays from it offline, turning pages with a tap or a foot pedal.

![Live mode: a song in three columns with chords, a personal note and chord diagrams](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/setDING/setDING/main/docs/screenshots/live.png)

- 🎸 **Made for the stage:** full-screen live mode, page turners, works completely offline
- 🎼 **ChordPro** songs and sets the whole band shares, with personal adjustments that never change the shared song
- 📱 **Android, Windows, Linux and the web**
- 🏠 **Self-hosted:** run the server yourself with Docker or as a single bundle
- 📖 **Open source** under the MIT License
